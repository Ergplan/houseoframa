House of Rama · the build, step by step

What happens when you say “read my bills”

This is the real sequence an agent goes through, in the order it goes through it, when a non-coder gives it one sentence. Left: what the agent does and says, in its own words. Right: what you see on screen at that moment. Under each: what it means for you. Twelve steps, about twenty minutes.

●“Make me something that reads my electricity bills, gives me the exact data printed on them, and keeps every bill in a database.”
