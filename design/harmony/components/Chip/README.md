# Chip

Small pills for status, codes and times. One or two words; state is carried by a word or sign, never by colour alone.

- default: neutral glass ("Auto: OFF" with `eg-dot--off`).
- `--accent`: positive or live ("Live updates" with the pulsing `eg-dot--live`, "26.19% saved").
- `--negative`, `--warning`: failed bids, delayed meter data, deviations.
- `--mono`: plant and meter codes (`N2UP0AEV0003`).
- `--time`: the larger time pill used at the end of timeline cards ("09:00 AM").
- `--solid`: counts beside a section title ("8 units").

Avatars (`eg-avatars` > `eg-avatar`) use initials, overlapped by 9px.
